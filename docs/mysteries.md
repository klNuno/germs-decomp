# Open questions

What this project wants to learn about Germs: Nerawareta Machi from its code. Each question says why it is still open and where in the code the answer should be. A question is ticked only when the code already answers it, with a link to the entry in [discoveries.md](discoveries.md) that cites the functions and addresses. What players and earlier projects already know: [community-knowledge.md](community-knowledge.md).

36 questions: 27 answered, 9 open, 5 of them partly answered.

## Time and days

- [x] 1. What does the "time control" cheat value at `0x801E6DB0` (600 normally) count? Answered: the number of screen refreshes per game minute, which gives the 6x speed players measured ([the clock](discoveries.md#-the-clock-counts-minutes-and-stops-at-day-99)).
- [ ] 2. What decides when each of the 32 emails arrives? Partly answered: the timed ones are a list in `mail_check_schedule`, twelve mails at eight times on days 1 to 4, four of them under story conditions ([the timed mails](discoveries.md#-the-timed-mails-are-written-in-the-code-for-days-1-to-4)). Open: the story-driven mails and the per-minute hook in `event_tick_hook`.
- [x] 3. Does the invasion move on by itself with time, or only through your actions? Answered: the clock never moves it. Five buildings have a day and hour from which they fall, applied only when you step out of the office into the town or reach their own door; the others fall as a chain when you free another building ([invasion schedule](discoveries.md#-time-alone-never-spreads-the-invasion-buildings-fall-on-a-schedule-when-you-pass-a-door)).
- [x] 4. Where are opening hours stored? Answered: there is no table; each door's script in the town tests the hour (game variable 62). Only the burger shop (08:00 to 21:59), the Italian restaurant (11:00 to 21:59) and `MPS` (05:00 to 23:59) close; one of the hospital's two doors, and its desk and exam rooms inside, keep 07:00 to 19:59 for a human, the other door does not ([opening hours](discoveries.md#-only-three-shops-close-at-night-the-opening-hours-sit-in-the-towns-door-scripts)). Only the burger shop's door was run in the emulator.
- [x] 5. What is the day parity flag for? Answered: the only code that reads it is the burger shop's, which secretly serves changed food at hours that swap between odd and even days; the town's door scripts also test it to pick which version of the burger shop, `FMR` and the Italian restaurant you enter ([the burger shop](discoveries.md#-the-burger-shop-secretly-serves-changed-food-and-even-days-swap-its-hours)). An even day itself was not played.

## Survival

- [ ] 6. Where are HP, fatigue and stamina? The emulator shows one energy value at `0x801E0710` that is your life ([energy at 0](discoveries.md#-energy-at-0-is-death-and-only-a-hit-to-the-head-makes-you-wake-up-changed)), drained faster by hours awake (`0x801E0746`, the fatigue players describe) and by torso damage ([energy](discoveries.md#-energy-drains-faster-the-longer-you-stay-awake)). Whether the status screen shows three values built from these needs the status screen code, and the food handler.
- [x] 7. What is the flag at `0x801E073C`? Answered: the "changed" (mutant) state, set when you wake up in the mine and cleared by the hospital's cure, seen in the emulator; it also sets every stat to 100 until the cure gives the real ones back ([being changed](discoveries.md#-being-changed-is-one-flag-a-pink-body-fast-healing-and-no-fatigue)).
- [x] 8. Which body part is which, and what does each level do? Answered: the order is head, arm, arm, torso, legs, checked on the HUD figure, and the torso multiplies the energy drain ([body parts](discoveries.md#-body-part-damage-heals-by-the-hour-unless-it-is-at-the-worst-level)); red or blue legs slow you to 90 % or 70 % and weaken your guard, the arm on the left of the figure lowers your hit chance, and the head's and the other arm's levels change nothing ([hurt legs and arm](discoveries.md#-hurt-legs-slow-you-down-and-one-hurt-arm-spoils-your-aim)).
- [x] 9. How much food is too much? Answered: the limit is a full bar; eating on it costs half the dish's value and puts its whole value on the torso as damage ([food](discoveries.md#-food-adds-a-fixed-amount-of-energy-and-eating-on-a-full-bar-hurts-your-torso)).
- [x] 10. What happens at energy 0? Answered: you fall; it is game over, unless an enemy hit your head since the last energy tick, which wakes you changed in the mine ([energy at 0](discoveries.md#-energy-at-0-is-death-and-only-a-hit-to-the-head-makes-you-wake-up-changed)).
- [x] 11. What do the chiropractor and the hospital heal? Answered: both heal only the body parts, never the energy bar. The chiropractor's 1, 3 and 6-hour courses take 60, 180 or 360 points off every part and cost that time ([the chiropractor](discoveries.md#-the-chiropractor-sells-time-1-3-or-6-hours-that-heal-as-much)); the hospital's injection clears every part at once and costs no time ([the hospital](discoveries.md#-the-hospital-heals-every-wound-at-once-for-free-and-without-losing-time)). Still open: what story flag lets a human reach the hospital's doctor.

## Combat

- [x] 12. How does the accuracy roll work? Answered: a roll of 0 to 99 against the hit chance, which three distances per weapon, a 20 % floor, one movement state and one arm adjust ([the hit roll](discoveries.md#-every-shot-is-a-roll-against-the-hit-chance-and-range-cuts-both-chance-and-damage)). Which movement states cost 10 % is still to see on screen.
- [x] 13. What do the five weapon class values hold? Answered: two halves, a level from 1 to 100 and the experience toward the next one, so `0x3B2A005D` is level 93; the blue wheel is the weapon's hit rate, and a class holds one to five weapons ([the blue wheel](discoveries.md#-the-blue-wheel-is-the-weapons-hit-rate-and-the-five-classes-are-levels-up-to-100)).
- [x] 14. How does the gun shop pick its six weapons? Answered: by the player level divided by 20, three bands and no fourth, always the same six weapons in a band; the baton, the M47 Dragon and the five special weapons are on no list, and a changed player (level 100) gets the top list ([the gun shop](discoveries.md#-the-gun-shop-has-three-fixed-lists-of-six-and-seven-weapons-are-never-sold)). Selling back is still to look at.
- [x] 15. What are the fields of the 18 `WEPN` records after offset 0x24? Answered: an extra energy cost, three requirements, the fire frame, accuracy, damage and three ranges; three fields are never read ([the weapon record](discoveries.md#-the-weapon-record-three-requirements-accuracy-damage-and-three-ranges)).

## People, police and mutation

- [x] 16. How many civilian kills get you arrested? Answered: 3 for the first arrest, 5 more for the second, counted only for civilians and checked whenever the office loads ([arrests](discoveries.md#-three-civilian-kills-get-you-arrested-and-five-more-the-second-time)). What the second arrest changes in the police station is still to see.
- [x] 17. Which people come back, and when? Answered: everyone, at every visit, since people are read again from the disc at each load; only a death script that writes a story value can keep one dead, if the place's scripts test that value; 55 of the 412 person records have one ([the dead come back](discoveries.md#-the-dead-come-back-when-you-return-unless-their-death-script-records-the-kill)).
- [x] 18. Where is each building's invasion state (0 to 4 on the office computer) stored, and what moves it? Answered: one game variable per building (9 to 27), worth 0, any other value, 10, 1000 or 2000; it also picks the version of the building that loads, and scene scripts, the change, the cure and the ending write it ([invasion state](discoveries.md#-each-buildings-invasion-state-is-one-number-and-the-computers-dots-show-it)).
- [x] 19. Is mutant play a dead end? Answered: yes. Every building switches to a changed version that sends no mail and leads nowhere, four places shut their doors, and the cure puts every building back ([being changed](discoveries.md#-being-changed-is-one-flag-a-pink-body-fast-healing-and-no-fatigue)); the gun shop has no second list, it only sees your level of 100 ([the gun shop](discoveries.md#-the-gun-shop-has-three-fixed-lists-of-six-and-seven-weapons-are-never-sold)).

## The ending

- [x] 20. What happens after the ending narration? Answered: the office's ending mode sets every building to its "at peace" state, heals you, sets the clock to 05:00, of the next day only if it was already 05:00 or later, and lets you play on; arrests and timed mails stop, death is always game over, and the buildings with no "at peace" version stay shut ([the ending](discoveries.md#-the-game-has-an-ending-a-narration-in-the-office-then-a-town-at-peace)). What the open buildings hold afterwards was not walked.

## Hidden and cut content

- [x] 21. Can the movie in `M.STR` and `D.STR` be played from the game? Answered: no, no code opens it and the game has no video decoder ([the movie](discoveries.md#-the-movie-on-the-disc-can-never-play)). What it was made for stays open.
- [x] 22. Does the game read the `\S\%s.S`, `\S\OFC.S` and `\S\TWN.S` files it names? Answered: no, the loader opens `SCE.S` and seeks through an offset table ([scene files](discoveries.md#-the-game-still-names-scene-files-the-disc-does-not-have)).
- [ ] 23. Were City Hall, the Yamasaki mansion and two South End buildings meant to be entered? The bus map marks them as buildings and City Hall has a door texture behind its arches. Partly answered: the 24 scenes of `SCE.S` are the office, the town, the waterway, mine level 2 and the 20 places the town's doors lead to, so none is left over for City Hall or the mansion (read in the town script). Open: the `B`, `F` and `M` area files, the door data in `C/CITY.*` and the bus stops.
- [x] 24. Can Naomi be hurt? She cannot be targeted, yet her profile text and a hit animation exist. Answered: no. She is not in the table of people the target picker scans, or in any scene's people at all: she is a stage model the scripts show and move, and her profile card can never open for the same reason ([the hit roll](discoveries.md#-every-shot-is-a-roll-against-the-hit-chance-and-range-cuts-both-chance-and-damage)). The hit animation was not looked at.
- [ ] 25. Were faces planned for everyone? Most people have no face texture. The people models in the area files and the code that applies their textures would show empty face slots or a shared blank.
- [ ] 26. Was the town once in colour? The rumour has no source. The clock code shades the outdoor fog through `SetFarColor` by time of day; any unused colour path in the town renderer, or colour data in `C/CITY.*`, would support it.
- [x] 27. What opens the profiles panel at `0x80182D34`? Answered: it is the lingering-thoughts card, opened by walking into a dead person and pressing CIRCLE, seen in the emulator ([the profile panel](discoveries.md#-the-profile-panel-is-the-lingering-thoughts-of-the-dead)).
- [x] 28. How does the Kouda reward get skipped? Answered: the reward needs a tally of exactly 7, which one talk with each of the three agents gives (1 + 2 + 4), and a visit to Kouda before the ending; the hospital agent is gone once the hospital's boss is beaten, and the warehouse agent can be counted again, so a second talk after the three meetings spoils the tally. The way to mine level 2 is not one way in the data ([Kouda's reward](discoveries.md#-koudas-reward-needs-an-agent-tally-of-exactly-7-before-the-ending)).

## Bugs

- [x] 29. Why can boss music start after the boss is dead? Answered: the boss track starts in the boss's talk set, run when you enter its talk zone, and the bosses of the power plant, `PSG`, the cathedral and the warehouses leave that zone on when they die; the talk then runs again at the cathedral and the warehouses, at the power plant only if its state was above 90, and never at `PSG`, whose death takes its state out of the talk's range ([boss music](discoveries.md#-boss-music-starts-in-the-bosss-talk-zone-and-four-bosses-leave-it-on-when-they-die)).
- [ ] 30. Why does the mine music keep playing during the Komuro fight? Partly answered: no stop call is skipped; mine level 1's music bank holds only the boss track, and the sound started on arrival is a held note that only leaving the place stops ([the mine's sound](discoveries.md#-mine-level-1-has-no-music-track-of-its-own-so-no-boss-fight-stops-its-sound)). Open: hearing that this note is the mine music players mean, and that the boss is Komuro.
- [ ] 31. Why does the game need a faster CPU in emulators? Partly answered: the game measures each frame's real length and scales movement by it, 30 frames a second at best ([frame timing](discoveries.md#-the-game-times-every-frame-so-a-slow-machine-makes-it-choppy-not-slow)), so a slow emulator makes it choppy. Open: a run at another emulated CPU speed, and why 190 % helps.
- [ ] 32. Why does the car snag on kerbs and tunnel edges? The car physics at `0x801AA360` and the collision reader at `0x8017A29C` hold the step height and the stop rule.
- [ ] 33. Does the analog pad bug come from the game or only from the BIOS routine? Partly answered: the game reads the pad only through `PadRead` and the BIOS routine and never asks the pad type; `0x801E6DA0` holds the pad words, not a handler ([the pad](discoveries.md#-the-game-never-asks-what-kind-of-pad-is-plugged-in)). Open: a run with an emulated analog pad.

## Data and formats

- [x] 34. How big is the town? Answered: a grid of 50 m blocks, 2,401 of them in the town itself, about 6 km², the area of a 2.5 km square, over about 4.75 by 4.9 km ([the town's size](discoveries.md#-the-town-is-a-grid-of-50-m-blocks-about-48-km-across)).
- [x] 35. What are the `SCE.S` DIR fields a to d, the 12-byte `AREA` triggers, `ENMY` and `ACTR`? Answered: four counts per directory entry (profile cards, stage models, weapons and items for the shared data; people, triggers, scripts and stat blocks for a version), the trigger's kind, scripts, wall flag and switch, and the person record's fields ([the scene data](discoveries.md#-the-scene-data-four-counts-per-place-door-triggers-and-people-records)). The `ENMY` fields beyond the id are still open.
- [x] 36. What is in the save? Answered: 462 bytes from five memory ranges (the game variables, the options, the player's status, the inventory and the position) and 2 for the save place, in a 508-byte buffer whose last 44 bytes stay zero, each quarter written twice with a checksum ([the save](discoveries.md#-a-save-holds-464-bytes-of-game-state-stored-twice-with-checksums)).
