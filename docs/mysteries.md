# Open questions

What this project wants to learn about Germs: Nerawareta Machi from its code. Each question says why it is still open and where in the code the answer should be. A question is ticked only when the code already answers it, with a link to the entry in [discoveries.md](discoveries.md) that cites the functions and addresses. What players and earlier projects already know: [community-knowledge.md](community-knowledge.md).

36 questions: 34 answered, 2 partly answered (30 and 31), whose rest cannot be settled here.

## Time and days

- [x] 1. What does the "time control" cheat value at `0x801E6DB0` (600 normally) count? Answered: the number of screen refreshes per game minute, which gives the 6x speed players measured ([the clock](discoveries.md#-the-clock-counts-minutes-and-stops-at-day-99)).
- [x] 2. What decides when each of the 32 emails arrives? Answered: each mail has one sender. Three are there at a new game; twelve are a list in `mail_check_schedule`, at eight times on days 1 to 4, four of them under story conditions; seventeen are sent by script command 13 in story scenes: the power plant's switch, the cathedral, the deaths of the hotel's boss, the coal mine's boss and person 0 in `OMH`, Kouda's reward, and three follow-ups when you leave the office a set number of days after a story step (one of them counted from the warehouse boss's death). The per-minute hook `event_tick_hook` only runs the trains ([the timed mails](discoveries.md#-the-timed-mails-are-written-in-the-code-for-days-1-to-4)). Only the power plant's three were seen arriving in the emulator.
- [x] 3. Does the invasion move on by itself with time, or only through your actions? Answered: the clock never moves it. Five buildings have a day and hour from which they fall, applied only when you step out of the office into the town or reach their own door; the others fall as a chain when you free another building ([invasion schedule](discoveries.md#-time-alone-never-spreads-the-invasion-buildings-fall-on-a-schedule-when-you-pass-a-door)).
- [x] 4. Where are opening hours stored? Answered: there is no table; each door's script in the town tests the hour (game variable 62). Only the burger shop (08:00 to 21:59), the Italian restaurant (11:00 to 21:59) and `MPS` (05:00 to 23:59) close; one of the hospital's two doors, and its desk and one of its exam rooms inside, keep 07:00 to 19:59 for a human, the other door and the other exam room do not ([opening hours](discoveries.md#-only-three-shops-close-at-night-the-opening-hours-sit-in-the-towns-door-scripts)). Only the burger shop's door was run in the emulator.
- [x] 5. What is the day parity flag for? Answered: the only code that reads it is the burger shop's, which secretly serves changed food at hours that swap between odd and even days; the town's door scripts also test it to pick which version of the burger shop, `FMR` and the Italian restaurant you enter ([the burger shop](discoveries.md#-the-burger-shop-secretly-serves-changed-food-and-even-days-swap-its-hours)). An even day itself was not played.

## Survival

- [x] 6. Where are HP, fatigue and stamina? Answered: they are three different things. HP is the damage of the five body parts, fatigue the hours awake (`0x801E0746`), stamina the energy at `0x801E0710` that is your life ([energy at 0](discoveries.md#-energy-at-0-is-death-and-only-a-hit-to-the-head-makes-you-wake-up-changed), [energy](discoveries.md#-energy-drains-faster-the-longer-you-stay-awake)). The status page draws the five wounds and the hours awake, never the energy, seen in the emulator ([the status page](discoveries.md#-the-status-page-shows-your-wounds-and-hours-awake-never-your-energy)).
- [x] 7. What is the flag at `0x801E073C`? Answered: the "changed" (mutant) state, set when you wake up in the mine and cleared by the hospital's cure, seen in the emulator; it also sets every stat to 100 until the cure gives the real ones back ([being changed](discoveries.md#-being-changed-is-one-flag-a-pink-body-fast-healing-and-no-fatigue)).
- [x] 8. Which body part is which, and what does each level do? Answered: the order is head, arm, arm, torso, legs, checked on the HUD figure, and the torso multiplies the energy drain ([body parts](discoveries.md#-body-part-damage-heals-by-the-hour-unless-it-is-at-the-worst-level)); red or blue legs slow you to 90 % or 70 % and weaken your guard, the arm on the left of the figure lowers your hit chance, and the head's and the other arm's levels change nothing ([hurt legs and arm](discoveries.md#-hurt-legs-slow-you-down-and-one-hurt-arm-spoils-your-aim)).
- [x] 9. How much food is too much? Answered: the limit is a full bar; eating on it costs half the dish's value and puts its whole value on the torso as damage ([food](discoveries.md#-food-adds-a-fixed-amount-of-energy-and-eating-on-a-full-bar-hurts-your-torso)).
- [x] 10. What happens at energy 0? Answered: you fall; it is game over, unless an enemy hit your head since the last energy tick, which wakes you changed in the mine ([energy at 0](discoveries.md#-energy-at-0-is-death-and-only-a-hit-to-the-head-makes-you-wake-up-changed)).
- [x] 11. What do the chiropractor and the hospital heal? Answered: both heal only the body parts, never the energy bar. The chiropractor's 1, 3 and 6-hour courses take 60, 180 or 360 points off every part and cost that time ([the chiropractor](discoveries.md#-the-chiropractor-sells-time-1-3-or-6-hours-that-heal-as-much)); the hospital's injection clears every part at once and costs no time ([the hospital](discoveries.md#-the-hospital-heals-every-wound-at-once-for-free-and-without-losing-time)). No story flag is needed: passing an exam room door stores the sum of your wounds, and the doctor and the bed treat you only if it is not zero, seen in the emulator.

## Combat

- [x] 12. How does the accuracy roll work? Answered: a roll of 0 to 99 against the hit chance, which three distances per weapon, a 20 % floor, two movement states and one arm adjust ([the hit roll](discoveries.md#-every-shot-is-a-roll-against-the-hit-chance-and-range-cuts-both-chance-and-damage)). Walking and side-stepping cost 10 %, seen in the emulator; standing and running do not (running read in the code).
- [x] 13. What do the five weapon class values hold? Answered: two halves, a level from 1 to 100 and the experience toward the next one, so `0x3B2A005D` is level 93; the blue wheel is the weapon's hit rate, and a class holds one to five weapons ([the blue wheel](discoveries.md#-the-blue-wheel-is-the-weapons-hit-rate-and-the-five-classes-are-levels-up-to-100)).
- [x] 14. How does the gun shop pick its six weapons? Answered: by the player level divided by 20, three bands and no fourth, always the same six weapons in a band; the baton, the M47 Dragon and the five special weapons are on no list, and a changed player (level 100) gets the top list ([the gun shop](discoveries.md#-the-gun-shop-has-three-fixed-lists-of-six-and-seven-weapons-are-never-sold)). Nothing can be sold back: the game has no money, and the shop refuses a third weapon when both hands are full, seen in the emulator.
- [x] 15. What are the fields of the 18 `WEPN` records after offset 0x24? Answered: an extra energy cost, three requirements, the fire frame, accuracy, damage and three ranges; three fields are never read ([the weapon record](discoveries.md#-the-weapon-record-three-requirements-accuracy-damage-and-three-ranges)).

## People, police and mutation

- [x] 16. How many civilian kills get you arrested? Answered: 3 for the first arrest, 5 more for the second, counted only for civilians and checked whenever the office loads ([arrests](discoveries.md#-three-civilian-kills-get-you-arrested-and-five-more-the-second-time)). The second arrest sets the police station's state (variable 17) to 100, its invaded version, and you wake up in its cell, seen in the emulator with the empty version loaded too; beating its boss and talking to the last human empties it, read in the scripts.
- [x] 17. Which people come back, and when? Answered: everyone, at every visit, since people are read again from the disc at each load; only a death script that writes a story value can keep one dead, if the place's scripts test that value; 55 of the 412 person records have one ([the dead come back](discoveries.md#-the-dead-come-back-when-you-return-unless-their-death-script-records-the-kill)).
- [x] 18. Where is each building's invasion state (0 to 4 on the office computer) stored, and what moves it? Answered: one game variable per building (9 to 27), worth 0, any other value, 10, 1000 or 2000; it also picks the version of the building that loads, and scene scripts, the change, the cure and the ending write it ([invasion state](discoveries.md#-each-buildings-invasion-state-is-one-number-and-the-computers-dots-show-it)).
- [x] 19. Is mutant play a dead end? Answered: yes. Every building switches to a changed version that sends no mail and leads nowhere, four places shut their doors, and the cure puts every building back ([being changed](discoveries.md#-being-changed-is-one-flag-a-pink-body-fast-healing-and-no-fatigue)); the gun shop has no second list, it only sees your level of 100 ([the gun shop](discoveries.md#-the-gun-shop-has-three-fixed-lists-of-six-and-seven-weapons-are-never-sold)).

## The ending

- [x] 20. What happens after the ending narration? Answered: the office's ending mode sets every building to its "at peace" state, heals you, sets the clock to 05:00, of the next day only if it was already 05:00 or later, and lets you play on; arrests and timed mails stop, death is always game over, and the buildings with no "at peace" version stay shut ([the ending](discoveries.md#-the-game-has-an-ending-a-narration-in-the-office-then-a-town-at-peace)). The sixteen open buildings hold 37 people, none hostile; the only infected one left is a hotel guest who only talks (read in the data; four of the buildings loaded in the emulator).

## Hidden and cut content

- [x] 21. Can the movie in `M.STR` and `D.STR` be played from the game? Answered: no, no code opens it and the game has no video decoder ([the movie](discoveries.md#-the-movie-on-the-disc-can-never-play)). What it was made for stays open.
- [x] 22. Does the game read the `\S\%s.S`, `\S\OFC.S` and `\S\TWN.S` files it names? Answered: no, the loader opens `SCE.S` and seeks through an offset table ([scene files](discoveries.md#-the-game-still-names-scene-files-the-disc-does-not-have)).
- [x] 23. Were City Hall, the Yamasaki mansion and two South End buildings meant to be entered? The bus map marks them as buildings and City Hall has a door texture behind its arches. Answered for the disc: no way in exists. The town's floor has door spots in front of 17 buildings that have nothing else: no scene, no area files, no script, no car destination, and stepping on one does nothing, seen in the emulator. The blue door is the town's common blue panel ([dead door spots](discoveries.md#-seventeen-buildings-have-a-door-spot-on-the-street-and-nothing-behind-it)). City Hall and the mansion are only candidates for two of them. Cannot be settled: whether they are among the 17 and which spots they would be, since no data names them, or whether an entry was planned before the disc was made.
- [x] 24. Can Naomi be hurt? She cannot be targeted, yet her profile text and a hit animation exist. Answered: no. She is not in the table of people the target picker scans, or in any scene's people at all: she is a stage model the scripts show and move, and her profile card can never open for the same reason ([the hit roll](discoveries.md#-every-shot-is-a-roll-against-the-hit-chance-and-range-cuts-both-chance-and-damage)). Her model has no hit animation: it holds three animations, all played by the scripts, seen in the emulator. Cannot be settled: what the fandom author saw in PSXPrev.
- [x] 25. Were faces planned for everyone? Most people have no face texture. Answered for the disc: no face is missing. A faceless head is modelled with no texture at all, there is no empty face slot or shared blank, and no code swaps textures; 14 of 201 people models have a face ([faces](discoveries.md#-faceless-people-were-built-without-a-face-not-left-blank)). Cannot be settled: whether faces were planned for everyone at an earlier stage, since only this disc exists.
- [x] 26. Was the town once in colour? The rumour has no source. Answered for the disc: no colour version is left. The town's palettes and vertex colours are grey, the fog is always grey, and the renderer would pass a coloured vertex through, but none exists ([the town's colour](discoveries.md#-no-colour-version-of-the-town-is-left-on-the-disc)). Cannot be settled: whether an earlier build was in colour, since only this disc exists.
- [x] 27. What opens the profiles panel at `0x80182D34`? Answered: it is the lingering-thoughts card, opened by walking into a dead person and pressing CIRCLE, seen in the emulator ([the profile panel](discoveries.md#-the-profile-panel-is-the-lingering-thoughts-of-the-dead)).
- [x] 28. How does the Kouda reward get skipped? Answered: the reward needs a tally of exactly 7, which one talk with each of the three agents gives (1 + 2 + 4), and a visit to Kouda before the ending; the hospital agent is gone once the hospital's boss is beaten, and the warehouse agent can be counted again, so a second talk after the three meetings spoils the tally. The way to mine level 2 is not one way: its door back to level 1 works, walked in the emulator ([Kouda's reward](discoveries.md#-koudas-reward-needs-an-agent-tally-of-exactly-7-before-the-ending)).

## Bugs

- [x] 29. Why can boss music start after the boss is dead? Answered: the boss track starts in the boss's talk set, run when you enter its talk zone, and the bosses of the power plant, `PSG`, the cathedral and the warehouses leave that zone on when they die; the talk then runs again at the cathedral and the warehouses, at the power plant only if its state was above 90, and never at `PSG`, whose death takes its state out of the talk's range ([boss music](discoveries.md#-boss-music-starts-in-the-bosss-talk-zone-and-four-bosses-leave-it-on-when-they-die)).
- [ ] 30. Why does the mine music keep playing during the Komuro fight? Partly answered: no stop call is skipped; mine level 1's music bank holds only the boss track, and the sound started on arrival is a looped note held on its own voice, which plays on under the boss track until the music player takes that voice, about 14 seconds in; the boss is Komuro, seen on its profile card ([the mine's sound](discoveries.md#-mine-level-1-has-no-music-track-of-its-own-so-no-boss-fight-stops-its-sound)). Cannot be settled here: that this note is the mine music players mean, and whether its end is audible; that needs listening, and the emulator ran with no sound output.
- [ ] 31. Why does the game need a faster CPU in emulators? Partly answered: the game measures each frame's real length and scales movement by it, 30 frames a second at best, so a slow emulator makes it choppy. The coal mine with mutants needs about 3.3 screen refreshes of CPU work a frame in PCSX-Redux and drops to 15 frames a second (measured); computed from that, not run, it would be back to 30 from about 1.67 times the CPU speed, so 190 % should clear it ([frame timing](discoveries.md#-the-game-times-every-frame-so-a-slow-machine-makes-it-choppy-not-slow)). Cannot be settled here: how the console and DuckStation compare with PCSX-Redux's timing, and DuckStation's own threshold; PCSX-Redux has no CPU-only speed setting to run it, and neither the console nor DuckStation was available.
- [x] 32. Why does the car snag on kerbs and tunnel edges? Answered: there is no step height, the car climbs any kerb but crawls at a third of its speed on the pavement; the snag is the stop rule, which undoes the whole move and cuts the pedals when one corner would leave the ground, with no sliding along. Backing up frees it, seen in the emulator ([the car](discoveries.md#-the-car-climbs-any-kerb-but-stops-dead-when-a-corner-leaves-the-ground)).
- [x] 33. Does the analog pad bug come from the game or only from the BIOS routine? Answered: from the BIOS routine, which copies the buttons only for a digital pad or a neGcon; the game reads the pad only through `PadRead` and never asks or sets the pad type. With an emulated pad in analog mode no button reached the game, and switching back to digital restored control at once ([the pad](discoveries.md#-the-game-never-asks-what-kind-of-pad-is-plugged-in)).

## Data and formats

- [x] 34. How big is the town? Answered: a grid of 50 m blocks, 2,401 of them in the town itself, about 6 km², the area of a 2.5 km square, over about 4.75 by 4.9 km ([the town's size](discoveries.md#-the-town-is-a-grid-of-50-m-blocks-about-48-km-across)).
- [x] 35. What are the `SCE.S` DIR fields a to d, the 12-byte `AREA` triggers, `ENMY` and `ACTR`? Answered: four counts per directory entry (profile cards, stage models, weapons and items for the shared data; people, triggers, scripts and stat blocks for a version), the trigger's kind, scripts, wall flag and switch, and the person record's fields ([the scene data](discoveries.md#-the-scene-data-four-counts-per-place-door-triggers-and-people-records)). The fighters' `ENMY` stat blocks hold the attack class, the reaction to a hit, damage, defence, accuracy, aim time, speed, the resisted class, the fire chance, the fire frame and three ranges; no enemy throws, nine are immune (read in the code and data).
- [x] 36. What is in the save? Answered: 462 bytes from five memory ranges (the game variables, the options, the player's status, the inventory and the position) and 2 for the save place, in a 508-byte buffer whose last 44 bytes stay zero, each quarter written twice with a checksum ([the save](discoveries.md#-a-save-holds-464-bytes-of-game-state-stored-twice-with-checksums)).
